The Remynd Certification Knowledge Base

A place for Remynd employees to prepare for tech certifications — cheatsheets, mock exams and study notes, all shared by colleagues who passed the exam before you.

It's open-source: anyone at Remynd can add material when they pass a new certification.

Heads up: materials here are made by colleagues and are NOT official sources. They are study aids only — always double-check against the relevant vendor's official documentation and exam guides. No guarantee of accuracy or exam success.

Contribute your own material

Just passed a certification? Share what helped you.

  1. Create a folder named after the certification (e.g. AZ900).
  2. Drop your resources inside (cheatsheets, mock exams, notes — HTML / PDF / etc.).
  3. Copy an existing index.html from PL300/ or DP900/ and edit it for your certification.
  4. Add a card for your certification on this landing page.
  5. Open a pull request. See CONTRIBUTING.md for details.
